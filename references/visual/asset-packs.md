## GitHub mirrors / candidates for cloning

- `KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-City-Builder-Bits-1.0`, `...Dungeon-Remastered-1.0`: official, exist (HEAD verified); licence/size unchecked; low relevance.

Community mirrors exist (e.g. `walterpalladino/godot-quaternius-ultimate-stylized-nature`, `trebeljahr/quaternius-showcase`, `ETdoFresh/kenney.nl` mirror, `shorepine/kenney`); not vetted for licence or size, so not recommended; download from the publisher pages instead.

## Notes
- Classic buildings are chunky and timber-heavy; of these packs only Quaternius Medieval Village and WizP come close. Plan to retexture to muted hand-painted tones.
- Godot 4.5: glTF import works directly; Quaternius lists glTF.
