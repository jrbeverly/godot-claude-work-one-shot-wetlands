# Wetlands (Alliance level 20-25 zone)

Source note: (S) = search-backed; rest general knowledge, unverified. No images were viewed.
**Correction to brief:** Lake Everstill is in Redridge Mountains, not the Wetlands (the Wetlands searches never mentioned it). Wetlands water features instead include Menethil Bay, Black Channel Marsh, Bluegill Marsh, Sundown Marsh, Mosshide Fen (names from general knowledge).

## Terrain shape
- (S) Large wet region below Stonewrought Dam, north of Loch Modan: small rivers, lakes and ponds, bordered by mountains on three sides, open to the sea on the west. Flat boggy lowlands cut by pools, low rises with ruins, mountain walls to the north and east, Thandol Span access from Arathi (S).

## Palette (approximate)
- Murky olive-green `#556650`, grey-green grass `#6E7468`, mud brown `#6A5A48`; water dark slate-teal `#4A5B66`; stone grey `#8A8E8A`.
- (S) Overcast sky makes land "a uniform gray": sky/fog `#8C9690`, low saturation, very low contrast; occasional warm lantern accents at Menethil.

## Vegetation
- (S) Mostly tall grasses and a scattering of trees; reeds, dead trees, mossy rocks, lily/marsh plants. Foliage low and sparse; the openness reads as bleak.

## Roads / paths
- Dirt road from Menethil Harbor winding east along high ground between pools to Dun Algaz and Loch Modan; old stone causeways/bridges and Thandol Span (large stone bridge to Arathi). Roads are narrow and dark against grass; fords and wooden docks.

## Architecture
- Menethil Harbor: walled port with (S) "gabled roofs and turrets tumbling over the edges"; grey stone, dark slate roofs, docks, ships and lighthouse-like towers. Dun Algaz: dwarven stone gate/fort at the Loch Modan pass. Ruined forts and orc/troll-camp-style tents elsewhere (Angerfang, Direforge Hill; general knowledge).

## Fog / sky / lighting
- (S) Gray mist cloaks the ground; constant overcast; dense seaborne fog at dawn and dusk. Fog starts close and is milky grey-green; shadows nearly absent. This is the zone where fog is the main visual feature.

## Scale
- Wide, flat, and empty: sense of isolation; buildings small at distance and swallowed by mist; Menethil's walls loom out of the fog.

## Landmarks
Menethil Harbor (S), Dun Algaz (S), Thandol Span (S), Stonewrought Dam (S, edge), Black Channel Marsh, Whelgar's Excavation Site, Raptor Ridge, Angerfang Encampment, Dun Modr, Ironbeard's Tomb.

## What makes it feel Classic
- Moodiness via fog and a restricted grey-green palette; clean simple pool shapes.
- Low, flat silhouettes with few tall vertical accents; hand-painted mud/grass ground blends.
- Isolation: a single road and scattered ruins.
