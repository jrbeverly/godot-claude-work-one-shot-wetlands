# Dun Morogh (dwarf/gnome starting zone)

Source note: (S) = search-backed snippet; rest is general knowledge, unverified. No images were viewed.

## Terrain shape
- High-altitude snowy mountain country: (S) "snow-swept land covered with forest and great pine trees", grey craggy mountains. Coldridge Valley is a sheltered bowl at the south-west; the road climbs through valleys, frozen lakes (Iceflow Lake, Frostmane/Gol'Bolar areas) and switchbacks up to Ironforge's mountain gate. Broad snow slopes, steep rocky ridges, tight valleys.

## Palette (approximate)
- Snow `#E8EEF4` with blue shadow `#B6C6DA`; pine dark green `#2F4A3A`; exposed rock grey-brown `#8A7A6A`; bare earth/road `#9A8672`.
- Warm accents: forge glow and window light `#E0A050`, dwarven red/brown timber `#7A4A2A`.
- Sky pale blue-grey `#C0CCDA`, bright and cold; snow-sky haze nearly the same value as the ground.

## Vegetation
- Conifers (pines/firs) with snow on boughs, bare bushes, frosted grass patches, dead stumps; little else. Trees cluster on slopes, leaving white open fields.

## Roads / paths
- Trampled earth/snow road runs from Coldridge through Anvilmar, Kharanos and on to Ironforge; darker brown line against white snow, curved with contours; stone bridges and tunnels.

## Architecture
- Dwarven: heavy stone and timber with thick roofs, round windows, forge chimneys; Kharanos is a hillside village around the Thunderbrew Distillery, forge and gnomish workshop (S). Anvilmar smithing enclave (S). Ironforge's gate is a massive carved mountain entrance.

## Fog / sky / lighting
- Constant snowfall at altitude (S); pale cold ambient light, blue shadows, whitish distance fog that merges mountain tops with sky. Warm windows pop against cold surroundings, particularly at night.

## Scale
- Mountains huge relative to tiny villages; vertical relief is the main scale cue; the road shrinking up a valley is the classic shot.

## Landmarks
Coldridge Valley/Anvilmar, Kharanos with Thunderbrew Distillery, Gol'Bolar Quarry, Frostmane Hold, Iceflow Lake, Amberstill Ranch, Helm's Bed Lake, Gates of Ironforge, Brewnall Village (S for Kharanos/Anvilmar/Brewnall).

## What makes it feel Classic
- Cold blue/white palette broken only by dark pines and warm window glow.
- Simple chunky mountain silhouettes with banded rock; very low-detail snow ground.
- Cosy hearth villages in a harsh landscape; falling snow particles as ambience.
