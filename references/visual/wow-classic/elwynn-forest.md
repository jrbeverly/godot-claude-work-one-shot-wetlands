# Elwynn Forest (human starting zone)

Source note: facts marked (S) came from search snippets (wowpedia/others); the rest is general knowledge of the original game and is unverified. No images were viewed.

## Terrain shape
- Broad, gently rolling temperate valley; low hills, shallow streams and pools, a few small lakes (Crystal Lake, Stonecairn Lake area). Slopes are soft; steeper rock only at edges: Northshire Valley to the north-west is a sheltered bowl, Burning Steppes foothills (red-brown) to the north-east.
- (S) "vast woodland of stately hardwoods ... just below the foothills of the Burning Steppes"; heartland of Stormwind.

## Palette (approximate)
- Grass/canopy mid greens `#5E8C3A`, lighter yellow-green sun patches `#8DB04A`, deep shade `#2F4A2A`.
- Dirt roads warm tan `#B8955E`; timber and stone buildings `#8A6A44` / `#8C8C86`; slate/tile roofs blue-grey or rust-red.
- Sky soft blue `#9DB8D4` with white clouds; haze slightly warm. Autumn-tinted trees appear in places.

## Vegetation
- Broadleaf oak-like trees with thick trunks and rounded canopy masses, often in loose stands with clearings; scattered pines near hills; ferns, bushes, flowers, tall grass tufts; vineyards and wheat near farms (Eastvale Logging Camp, Brackwell Pumpkin Patch, Stone Cairn Lake farms).

## Roads / paths
- A clear tan dirt road runs from Northshire down through Goldshire and on to Stormwind; branches to Westbrook, Eastvale, Stonefield, Tower of Azora. Roads curve with terrain, edged by fences, signposts, stone bridges.

## Architecture
- Human timber-frame and plaster cottages, steep grey-blue or red-tile roofs, stone foundations, chimneys, fences, wagons, barrels. Northshire Abbey: a stone church complex (Main Hall, Hall of Arms, Library Wing, S) with tall roofs and blue banners. Goldshire: Lion's Pride Inn at the central crossroads, blacksmith, Stormwind-blue/gold banners. Stormwind's walls and towers are visible at the south edge.

## Fog / sky / lighting
- Bright, soft, daytime-friendly: light blue sky, white cloud puffs, long soft haze that tints distant hills blue-green. Dappled warm light under trees; gentle blue-ish shade. Night is deep blue, not black.

## Scale
- Valley-scale zone with villages a short walk apart; trees ~3-4x character height; cottages low and wide; Stormwind gate a huge visible scale marker.

## Landmarks
Northshire Abbey, Goldshire (Lion's Pride Inn), Fargodeep Mine, Echo Ridge Mine, Westbrook Garrison, Eastvale Logging Camp, Jerod's Landing, Crystal Lake, Tower of Azora, the Stormwind road and gate.

## What makes it feel Classic
- Cozy, pastoral, safe; green dominates and big readable tree clumps frame roads.
- Cottages are chunky with oversized roofs; textures painterly, no fine detail.
- Long soft blue haze over low hills; a single obvious road threads everything.
- Sparse props (fence, hay, barrel, cart) around each building.
