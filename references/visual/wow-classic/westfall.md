# Westfall (human level 10-15 zone)

Source note: (S) = search-backed snippet; rest general knowledge, unverified. No images were viewed.

## Terrain shape
- (S) "rolling pastures and farms, barren with drought and maltreatment"; coastal south-western Eastern Kingdoms. Low, wide, smooth hills and open plains; coast cliffs and beaches on the west; Dagger Hills and the Deadmines area in the south-west; Moonbrook ruined town in a shallow valley; the Jangolode Mine in the east.

## Palette (approximate)
- Dry grass `#B8A050` to straw `#C8B070`, olive `#8E8A44` in dips; bare dirt `#A5855A`; weathered wood greys `#7A6A52`; barn/roof red `#A5603A`; Defias red-brown accents.
- Sky warm pale blue to hazy cream `#D8C890` near horizon; sun-bleached and slightly desaturated.

## Vegetation
- Sparse: scattered small trees, scrubby bushes, patches of tall grass, abandoned crop rows, pumpkins, wheat; very open skyline. Few forests (Furlbrow's farm, Jangolode area have more).

## Roads / paths
- Long dirt road looping from Sentinel Hill past farms to Moonbrook and the Deadmines and north to Elwynn / west coast; lighter tan, often straight-ish across plains with gentle curves; fence lines and mill/farm tracks.

## Architecture
- Farmsteads: wood-plank barns, windmills, sheds, silos, fences, scarecrows (S: Furlbrow's Pumpkin Farm, Alexston, Saldean's, Jansen Stead, Molsen). Sentinel Hill: stone watch tower, wooden palisade/barracks (S: main hub). Many buildings visibly ruined; Defias camps with rough tents/wood shacks. Lighthouse at Westfall coast (Westfall Lighthouse; general knowledge).

## Fog / sky / lighting
- Bright warm daylight, dusty low-contrast haze, long sight lines across open plain; no heavy fog. Dusk/dawn orange glow reads strongly on the straw palette.

## Scale
- Wide open vistas; farms scattered a minute apart; Sentinel Hill tower and windmills as distance markers.

## Landmarks
Sentinel Hill, Moonbrook & the Deadmines entrance (S), Furlbrow's Pumpkin Farm, Saldean's Farm, Alexston Farmstead, Westfall Lighthouse, Gold Coast Quarry, Jangolode Mine, Dagger Hills.

## What makes it feel Classic
- Warm ochre monochrome with few punch colours (barn red, sky blue).
- Wide, sparse composition; gentle hills; simple fence/barn shapes.
- A zone with a story: neglect and abandonment shown through restraint, not clutter.
